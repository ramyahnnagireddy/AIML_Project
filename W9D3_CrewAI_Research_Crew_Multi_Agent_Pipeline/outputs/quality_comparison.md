# W9D3 Research Comparison

- **Topic:** Applications, benefits, risks, and limitations of AI in healthcare
- **Model:** Ollama `qwen2.5:3b`
- **Baseline report:** `baseline_report.md`
- **Web-enhanced report:** `web_enhanced_report.md`
- **Retrieved source leads:** `retrieved_sources.json`
- **Retrieved results:** 5

## Comparison method

Both reports address the same topic using the same local model and three-agent sequential CrewAI workflow. The baseline run uses no retrieved search results. The enhanced run receives Wikipedia search-result titles, snippets, and URLs.

The comparison is a qualitative review of the saved outputs, not a controlled quantitative evaluation.

## Baseline report

**Strengths**

- Provides a structured assessment of AI applications, benefits, risks, and limitations.
- Identifies research gaps involving bias, mental healthcare, implementation costs, and regulation.
- Offers recommendations for further investigation and practical implementation.

**Weaknesses**

- Some recommendations are broad and would benefit from specific supporting evidence.
- The report's claims are not consistently accompanied by verifiable source links.

## Web-enhanced report

**Strengths**

- Includes specific application examples, including personalized medicine, computer-aided diagnosis, and AI-enabled medical devices.
- Discusses cybersecurity, equity, patient safety, data quality, privacy, costs, and human oversight.
- Uses retrieved healthcare-related source leads to make the discussion more topic-specific.

**Weaknesses**

- The retrieved results are search-result snippets, not verified full-text evidence.
- The report does not consistently link individual factual claims to their supporting sources.
- Some recommendations remain general, and claims about diagnostic improvements require independent verification.

## Reviewer conclusion

The web-enhanced report is more explicit about several healthcare application areas and provides a clearer connection to retrieved source leads. The baseline report, however, gives more explicit attention to certain research gaps, including bias mitigation, implementation costs, and regulatory options.

The observed difference is therefore a change in emphasis and specificity, rather than proof of overall quality improvement. Both reports need stronger claim-level citations and verification against authoritative sources. No numerical quality improvement is claimed because no standardized scoring rubric or independent factual verification was performed.

## Evidence limitations

Wikipedia search-result titles and snippets are discovery aids, not sufficient evidence to validate every claim. The retrieved sources should be checked directly before using the report for decisions or making clinical claims. This comparison evaluates report structure and content only; it does not establish clinical accuracy or safety.
