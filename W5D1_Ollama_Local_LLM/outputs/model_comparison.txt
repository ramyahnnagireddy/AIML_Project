**What is Overfitting?**

Overfitting is a common problem in machine learning and artificial intelligence. It occurs when a model is too complex and learns the noise in the training data, rather than the underlying patterns. As a result, the model performs well on the training data but poorly on new, unseen data.

Think of it like trying to fit a square peg into a round hole. The model is trying to fit the training data too closely, rather than finding a general solution that works well on a variety of data.

**Example:**

Imagine you're trying to predict house prices based on features like number of bedrooms, square footage, and location. If you create a model that's too complex, with too many features and interactions, it might fit the training data perfectly, but it will likely perform poorly on new data that doesn't have those exact features.
