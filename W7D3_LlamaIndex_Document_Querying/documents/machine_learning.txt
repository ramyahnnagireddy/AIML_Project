Machine learning enables computers to learn patterns from examples instead of relying only on explicitly programmed rules. Supervised learning uses labelled data and includes classification and regression. Classification predicts categories, such as spam or not spam. Regression predicts continuous values, such as house prices. Unsupervised learning finds patterns in unlabelled data and includes clustering. Data is commonly divided into training and testing sets. Accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and mean squared error are examples of evaluation metrics.
