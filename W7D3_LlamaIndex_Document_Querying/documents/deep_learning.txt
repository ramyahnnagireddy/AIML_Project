Deep learning is a branch of machine learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers. Artificial neural networks contain interconnected units called neurons. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are commonly used for image classification and computer vision. Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) were designed to process sequences. Transformers use attention mechanisms and are widely used in language tasks. Deep learning often requires substantial data and computational resources. Overfitting occurs when a model learns training data too closely and performs poorly on unseen data.
